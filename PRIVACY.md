# Google Home Camera Grid privacy policy

Google Home Camera Grid arranges and monitors playback of camera tiles on Google Home for web (`home.google.com`). Google Home provides the camera streams and handles sign-in.

The extension reads the Google Home camera grid page to identify the home and camera tiles, show camera names in its controls, and check playback status. To remember your layout, it saves the home’s page identifier, your selected cameras and their order, and your display and recovery preferences in local Chrome extension storage. The selection and order use identifiers derived from the camera names. It also saves the retry count, timing, and home key in the tab’s session storage to limit repeat reloads.

The extension uses this information on your device to arrange the grid and recover stalled feeds. It does not capture, record, store, or transmit camera footage or audio. It does not access sign-in credentials or send information it handles to the developer or any third party. It does not use analytics, sell data, or use data for advertising or credit decisions.

To remove saved layout preferences, uninstall the extension from Chrome. Closing the Google Home tab clears its recovery session data.

For questions about this policy, use the [project’s issue tracker](https://github.com/frodare/google-home-camera-grid/issues). Please keep camera names, footage, and other private details out of public issues.
